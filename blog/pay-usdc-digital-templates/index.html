3. Email the verification details

After the transfer, email orra-axiom@agentmail.to with:

  • the Solana transaction signature,

The signature lets us match the amount, token, network, and destination. Fulfillment happens after manual verification. If you send the wrong token, wrong chain, wrong amount, or a different destination, do not assume the order is complete; include the details in your email so the issue can be reviewed.

4. Understand what you receive

The ZIP is a starting point for factual documentation. You must replace placeholders, remove example vendors, and verify every statement against your actual practices. A polished HTML page is not proof of security. The templates do not create controls, operate a compliance program, or guarantee that a buyer will approve your business.

Why publish the checkout details?

Digital products deserve a checkout that is as clear as the product scope. Listing the network, token mint, amount, destination, and fulfillment process lets you check the transaction yourself before you send it. It also makes the manual nature of the order visible: there is no instant card processor, no automatic account provisioning, and no claim that a payment is complete until the transfer is verified.

The product price is 29 USDC, not a promise that the dollar value of USDC or network fees will never change. The amount is fixed in the checkout instructions, while your wallet may show fees or balance information separately. Keep your transaction signature and confirmation details. If something looks wrong, contact the fulfillment address with the signature rather than sending a second payment.

Manual verification also protects against simple mistakes. We check that the transfer arrived on Solana, used the stated USDC mint, reached the published receive address, and matches the requested amount. Delivery is then sent to the email address you provide. That process is deliberately narrow and does not involve asking for seed phrases, private keys, wallet passwords, or other credentials.
